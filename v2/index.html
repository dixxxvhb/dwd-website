Questions from
the wings.

Is it too late to join Season One?
Not yet. Our June audition set the core of the company, but placement is open in all three tracks. Express interest and Dixon will reach out personally to talk about your dancer and where they fit.
Is there another audition?
No formal audition. Placement happens through a conversation and, when it helps, a visit to class. Seeing how your dancer moves and how they take a correction tells us more than one nervous morning ever could.
What happens after I express interest?
Dixon reaches out personally, usually within a day or two. You will talk through your dancer's training history, which track fits, and what the season looks like. There is no fee to express interest.
Which track is right for my dancer?
Prep is ages 5 to 8 and builds the foundation. Elite is ages 8 to 12 and adds hours, styles, and a bigger competition load. Pro is ages 12 and up and trains at a pre-professional level. Age is a starting point, not a rule. Placement is about where your dancer will grow fastest.
What does it cost?
Tuition is $195, $310, or $360 a month depending on track, plus a one-time $50 registration and a $25 monthly admin fee during the season. Choreography is included. Costume and competition fees bill as monthly deposits that pay down the real totals from day one, and final balances bill when costumes are ordered and the competition slate locks.
What does a training week look like?
Prep trains Monday evenings plus Thursday company work, about 4 hours a week. Elite trains Tuesday through Thursday, about 8 hours. Pro trains Tuesday through Thursday, about 10, including a weekly solo hour. Every track shares Thursday company choreography, because the company dancing together is the point.
We would be joining after the season starts. Is my dancer behind?
The season is built in layers. New dancers are placed into routines as they join, and choreography is structured so a dancer starting in late summer is not behind. The sooner we talk, the easier that is.
My dancer has never competed. Is ProSeries a fit?
Maybe, and that is an honest maybe. ProSeries is a competitive training company, so the stage is the goal. Nobody starts out competition-ready, and Prep and Elite exist to build that dancer. If your kid loves to dance and wants to be pushed, express interest and let's talk.
Where do you train?
Exchange Dance Academy, 7409 Chancery Lane, Orlando. Evening classes, Monday through Thursday.
Who teaches?
Dixon leads every track and builds the choreography. Rotation faculty in hip hop, tap, acro, and musical theatre round out the training. Meet the whole team on the Teachers page.
