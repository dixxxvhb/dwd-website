## Appendix: proposed privacy policy wording (item 8)
- What We Collect, first sentence: "When you fill in a form on this site (the ProSeries interest
  form, the contact form, the episode recap signup, or a drop-in class booking), we collect what
  you type: your name, email and phone, your dancer's name and date of birth, and for a booking,
  that you accepted the Drop-In Class Waiver."
- Usage: "We also count how the site gets used: pages visited, time on page, how far you scroll,
  button taps, whether a form was started or hit an error, how fast pages load, and the site or
  campaign that sent you. No cookies. Your browser keeps a random ID so repeat visits count once;
  it is never attached to anything you submit."
- How we use it: "We use it to run DWD classes and programs and to talk with you about them. We
  never sell or rent it. We share it only with the services that run the site for us: Supabase
  stores it, and Stripe receives your email when you pay."
- New heading "Videos and Fonts": "Choreography videos come from YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode
  and only load when you tap play; until then you see a still image served by YouTube. Our fonts
  load from Google Fonts. Like any website, both see your IP address."
- Plaid: "Dance With Dixon uses Plaid to connect its own business bank accounts to our
  bookkeeping. Families never link a bank account through this site."
- Children: "We collect a child's name and date of birth only from a parent, through these forms."
- Last updated: the ship date.
